The organization faced inconsistent data quality across multiple platforms, leading to reporting errors and duplicated efforts between cross-functional teams. Without automated visualization, leadership lacked a 'single source of truth,' making it difficult to identify bottlenecks or implement data-driven process improvements."

Leveraged Lean principles to standardize inconsistent data sources and developed automated Power BI and Excel dashboards, significantly reducing manual reporting efforts and providing leadership with enhanced operational visibility for faster decision-making.

Post-launch analysys we eliminated manual reporting bottlenecks, improved data accuracy, and enabled faster, more informed strategic decision-making.
